SAP QC Results · pair-sample-3

Faithfulness check of 72 generated SAPs (pair-sample-3) against their protocol PDFs, run on 28 October 2026 with the pipeline in qc-pipeline/ (design: SAP QC Pipeline; earlier samples: pair-sample-1 and pair-sample-2). 11 of the 72 protocols also appear in an earlier sample (6 in pair-sample-1, 5 in pair-sample-2), with newly generated SAPs; the other 61 are new. This page reports token consumption, retrieval coverage (Recall@k) and accuracy, then compares the three samples.

Read the accuracy numbers as provisional.
  • Judge not independent. The LLM judge is gpt-5.6-luna, the generator's reviewer model (plan decision D6 overridden), so it may share the reviewer's blind spots.
  • 55% of judged facts were settled by JEV alone, without a verbatim quote (D9 relaxed). Those count as correct; their error rate is not yet measured.
  • The judge has not been validated. Phase P3 (planted errors, ~100 human gold labels, thresholds for k, τ and JEV's acceptance) has not run.

Who decided the verdicts
JEV accepted, no quote: 55% of judged factsLLM judge

JEV only ever accepts supported, so every error comes from LLM-judged facts: 2,524 errors in 15,493 LLM-judged facts (83.7% accurate). If JEV's accepted verdicts were 3% wrong, accuracy would fall by about 1.7% points.

"Not reported" facts are the weak spot

Absence facts are wrong 17.6% of the time strictly (10.5% lenient), over 2,672 judged: the SAP says an item is not reported, and the protocol reports it. In the random sample, absence facts are 82.5% accurate against 93.6% for facts that state something. That is still the weakest kind of fact, but less wrong than in pair-sample-1 (22.4%) and pair-sample-2 (23.5%). Citation precision (cited pages the LLM judge said support the fact) is 22.6%.

How well the triggers find errors

The triggers put 33,716 of 36,264 facts (93%) in the flagged set, almost all of them, so accT ≈ accR. The unflagged facts that were sampled (760) are wrong 4.3% of the time, well below flagged ones (7.4%). That is a clearer separation than in pair-sample-1 (7.0% vs 6.5%) or pair-sample-2 (8.1% vs 6.9%), but with nearly every fact flagged, the triggers still do little filtering. 6. Next steps

Validate the judge (P3)
Planted errors and ~100 human gold labels, scored separately for JEV-accepted and LLM-judged facts. This is what turns these numbers into reportable ones.
Catch unreadable text before judging
The garbled-text check missed jama.2025.21738's /0/1/2…/i255 glyph codes, so its SAP was judged against unreadable pages, and an image-only PDF (jama.2020.0592) crashed the index instead of being OCR'd or skipped. Both protocols had to be removed from the sample.
Parse every citation form
jama.2024.2302 writes citations as p2; 114 of its 150 went unparsed, which removes the document from coverage.
Freeze a sharper trigger set
COMPOUND and CRITICAL flag nearly everything in all three samples. Trimming them before freezing (end of P2) would make accT and the miss rate informative.
