SAP QC Results · pair-sample-2

Faithfulness check of 100 generated SAPs (pair-sample-2) against their protocol PDFs, run on 25 October 2026 with the pipeline in qc-pipeline/ (design: SAP QC Pipeline; first sample: SAP QC Results, pair-sample-1). The two samples share no documents. This page reports token consumption, retrieval coverage (Recall@k) and accuracy, then compares the two samples.

Read the accuracy numbers as provisional.
  • Judge not independent. The LLM judge is gpt-5.6-luna, the generator's reviewer model (plan decision D6 overridden), so it may share the reviewer's blind spots.
  • 53% of judged facts were settled by JEV alone, without a verbatim quote (D9 relaxed). Those count as correct; their error rate is not yet measured.
  • The judge has not been validated. Phase P3 (planted errors, ~100 human gold labels, thresholds for k, τ and JEV's acceptance) has not run.

Who decided the verdicts
JEV accepted, no quote: 53% of judged factsLLM judge

JEV only ever accepts supported, so every error comes from LLM-judged facts: 3,119 errors in 21,107 LLM-judged facts (85.2% accurate). If JEV's accepted verdicts were 3% wrong, accuracy would fall by about 1.6% points.

"Not reported" facts are the weak spot

Absence facts are wrong 23.5% of the time strictly (15.3% lenient), over 2,920 judged: the SAP says an item is not reported, and the protocol reports it. In the random sample, absence facts are 76.1% accurate against 94.0% for facts that state something. Citation precision (cited pages the LLM judge said support the fact) is 22.2%.

How well the triggers find errors

The triggers put 44,318 of 47,494 facts (93%) in the flagged set, almost all of them, so accT ≈ accR. The unflagged facts that were sampled (943) are wrong 8.1% of the time, about as often as flagged ones (6.9%): as a filter the trigger set does not yet separate risky facts from safe ones. 6. Next steps

Validate the judge (P3)
Planted errors and ~100 human gold labels, scored separately for JEV-accepted and LLM-judged facts. This is what turns these numbers into reportable ones.
Recover the two failed SAPs
Retry or split a judge call whose JSON is truncated, and give extract_pdf.py a fallback for PDFs that exceed its per-stream token limit.
Freeze a sharper trigger set
COMPOUND and CRITICAL flag nearly everything in both samples. Trimming them before freezing (end of P2) would make accT and the miss rate informative.
