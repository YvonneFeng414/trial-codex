# Statistical Analysis Plan

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Background and Rationale

Vitamin-D deficiency is common in critically ill patients and has been associated in cited observational literature with mortality and other adverse outcomes. This randomized trial evaluates correction of deficiency with high-dose oral cholecalciferol; the protocol states that no prospective ICU trial had evaluated clinical outcomes of this treatment. *(Source: Synopsis and Introduction, pages 2, 5-8)*

### 1.2 Objectives

**Primary Objectives**

- Determine whether high-dose oral vitamin D is beneficial in vitamin-D-deficient critically ill patients and whether it affects hospital length of stay. *(Source: Introduction—Objective, page 5)*

**Secondary Objectives**

- Evaluate ICU stay; mortality in ICU, in hospital, at day 28, and at month 6; day-7 vitamin-D repletion; biochemical and inflammatory measures; mechanical-ventilation duration; cardiac measures; and TISS-28 scores. *(Source: Synopsis and Outcome Variables, pages 2, 8-9)*

**Exploratory Objectives**

Not reported in protocol: no objective is labelled exploratory. *(Source: pages 1-31 reviewed)*

## 2. Study Methods

### 2.1 Trial Design

- **Design Type:** Prospective, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, single-centre trial at the Medical University of Graz, involving participating medical, cardiothoracic, neurologic and coronary-care ICUs. *(Source: Synopsis and Study design, pages 2, 9)*
- **Allocation Ratio:** 1:1 (480 patients: 240 in each group). *(Source: Synopsis and Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, pages 3, 9)*
- **Intervention Summary:** Enteral (nasogastric in ICU) or oral cholecalciferol (Oleovit D3; manufacturer: Fresenius Kabi Austria GmbH) versus identical colour-, smell-, and texture-matched placebo. *(Source: Synopsis, pages 3-4)*
  - Vitamin-D group: one 540,000-IU loading dose in 45 mL herbal oil, followed by 90,000 IU monthly (equivalent to 3,000 IU/day) through month 6; stated treatment duration is 5 months. *(Source: Synopsis, pages 3-4)*
  - Placebo group: same 45-mL oil volume at loading and respective monthly placebo doses. *(Source: Synopsis, pages 3-4)*

### 2.2 Randomization

- **Method:** After inclusion, patients are randomized to vitamin D or placebo using the web-based randomization tool at randomizer.at. *(Source: Methods of procedure, page 9)*
- **Stratification Factors:** Not reported in protocol. *(Source: pages 1-31 reviewed)*
- **Minimization or Blocking:** Not reported in protocol. *(Source: pages 1-31 reviewed)*

### 2.3 Sample Size

- **Planned Sample Size:** 480 patients, approximately 240 per group; 234 per group is the calculated requirement before inflation. *(Source: Synopsis and Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, pages 3, 9)*
- **Outcome Used for Calculation:** Hospital stay from ICU admission. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Outcome Type:** Continuous duration in days. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Assumed Control-Arm Value:** Hospital stay of 14 days. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Assumed Treatment-Arm Value:** 12 days, corresponding to a planned 2-day reduction. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Target Difference / Effect Size:** Reduction of 2 days (14%). *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Variability Assumption:** Standard deviation of 7 days. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Alpha (Significance Level):** 0.05, reported for the sample-size calculation only. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Power:** 0.80. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Statistical Test Assumed:** Mann-Whitney U test, with a logarithmic model for the sample-size calculation. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Required Number of Events:** Not applicable: the calculation is for hospital-stay duration, not an event-driven design. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Non-Inferiority / Equivalence Margin:** Not applicable: the calculation targets a reduction in hospital stay. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Dropout / Non-Adherence Inflation:** Although 234 per group are needed, approximately 240 per group will be enrolled because few consent withdrawals/dropouts are expected. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Design Effect / Clustering:** Not applicable: this is an individually randomized single-centre trial. *(Source: Synopsis and Study design, pages 2, 9)*
- **Basis for Assumptions:** Hospital-stay data from 2008 among critically ill patients staying at least 48 hours in the concerned ICUs. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Sample Size Re-Estimation / Amendments:** Not reported in protocol. *(Source: pages 1-31 reviewed)*
- **Software Used for Calculation:** Not reported in protocol. *(Source: pages 1-31 reviewed)*
- **Sample Size Rationale:** Power to detect the stated reduction in the primary hospital-stay outcome. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*

### 2.4 Hypothesis Framework

The stated calculation framework targets a reduction in hospital length of stay with vitamin D. Formal null and alternative hypotheses, the final-analysis comparison, and a hierarchy for endpoint testing are not reported in protocol. *(Source: Outcome Variables and Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, pages 8-9; pages 1-31 reviewed)*

### 2.5 Statistical Interim Analyses

- **Interim Analyses Planned:** No interim efficacy or futility analysis is reported. A data-safety review is proposed after 1 year or 100 patients; Version 2.0 specifies a first review after 100 patients and second after 250 patients. *(Source: Data safety review and Version 2.0 amendment, pages 10, 14)*
- **Stopping Guidelines:** Not reported in protocol. *(Source: pages 1-31 reviewed)*
- **Alpha Spending or Adjustment:** Not reported in protocol. *(Source: pages 1-31 reviewed)*

### 2.6 Timing of Analyses

- **Final Analysis Timing:** Not reported in protocol. Original planning states 24 months of recruitment and 30 months total study duration; database-lock and report timing are not reported. *(Source: Synopsis and Study duration, pages 2-3, 9; pages 1-31 reviewed)*
- **Outcome Assessment Schedule:** Screening is immediately after ICU admission/day 1; treatment begins on day 0 when eligibility is available. Most laboratory assessments are measured immediately on days 0, 3, 7, and day 28 if feasible/still hospitalized; IL-6 and cathelicidin may be measured from frozen specimens at study end. SAPS II, TISS-28, vital status, and morbidity are derived from MEDOCS source data. After discharge, vital status is assessed by telephone at day 28 and month 6. Version 2.0 adds month-6 follow-up, and Version 3.0 adds month-24 follow-up. *(Source: Synopsis, Methods of procedure, and amendments, pages 3, 9, 14-15)*

## 4. Trial Population

### 4.1 Screening Data

Serum 25(OH)D is measured in ICU-admitted patients on day 1. Following written informed consent, a screening number is assigned in ascending order. Cardiothoracic-surgery patients may consent preoperatively when a 48-hour ICU stay is anticipated; procedures for patients unable to consent are to be discussed with the ethics committee. *(Source: Methods of procedure, page 9)*

### 4.2 Eligibility Criteria Summary

- Inclusion criteria: age at least 18 years, expected ICU stay at least 48 hours, and 25(OH)D no greater than 20 ng/mL. *(Source: Synopsis and Inclusion criteria, pages 3, 10)*
- Exclusion criteria: total calcium >2.65 mmol/L or ionized calcium >1.35 mmol/L; ileus or residual gastric volume >400 mL; recent (within 1 year) kidney stones; tuberculosis or sarcoidosis; pregnancy/lactation; or unsuitability including psychiatric disease, prisoner status, living abroad/remote from clinic, or moribund condition at screening. *(Source: Exclusion criteria, page 10)*
- Women of childbearing age require a negative pregnancy test. *(Source: Women of childbearing age—pregnancy tests, page 10)*

### 4.3 Recruitment

Adults admitted to the University Hospital Graz medical, surgical, and neurological ICUs are the study population; the detailed design also names cardiothoracic and coronary-care ICUs. Recruitment was planned to start in April 2010 and remain active for 24 months. *(Source: Synopsis and Study design, pages 2-3, 9)*

### 4.4 Withdrawal and Follow-up

- **Withdrawal Levels:** A participant who subsequently declines participation after enrolment while unable to consent will not be considered in the analysis. Other withdrawal levels are not reported in protocol. *(Source: Methods of procedure, page 9; pages 1-31 reviewed)*
- **Withdrawal Data Collection:** Not reported in protocol. After hospital discharge, vital status is obtained by telephone at day 28 and month 6. At month 6 and month 24, approximately 80% have telephone/CRF follow-up and approximately 20% personal follow-up with a blood sample as on days 0, 3, 7 and 28, Timed Up & Go testing, DXA bone-mineral-density/body-composition assessment (about 10 µSv), and the relevant CRF. *(Source: Synopsis, Version 2.0 and Version 3.0 amendments, pages 3, 14-15)*
- **Reasons for Withdrawal:** Consent withdrawal is anticipated; no other withdrawal reasons are reported. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9; pages 1-31 reviewed)*

### 4.5 Baseline Characteristics

- **Baseline Variables:** Most day-0 standard-chemistry measures are obtained immediately, including creatinine, albumin, calcium, phosphate, magnesium, CRP, PCT, NT-proBNP, urinary calcium, 25(OH)D, 1,25(OH)2D, PTH, and bone-turnover markers. SAPS II, TISS-28, vital status, and morbidity are derived from MEDOCS source data. IL-6 and cathelicidin may be measured at study end from frozen blood specimens. *(Source: Synopsis, page 3)*
- **Summary Methods:** Not reported in protocol. *(Source: pages 1-31 reviewed)*

## 5. Analysis

### 5.1 Primary Outcomes

**Outcome: Hospital length of stay** *(Source: Synopsis and Outcome Variables, pages 2, 8)*

- **Definition:** Hospital stay; the power calculation defines its start as ICU admission. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Measurement Units:** Days. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** From ICU admission through hospital stay; no discharge/censoring rule is reported. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9; pages 1-31 reviewed)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** A logarithmic model is stated for the sample-size calculation only; no final derivation or transformation is reported. *(Source: Statistical aspects/Power analysis/Sample size calculation, page 9)*

### 5.2 Secondary Outcomes

**Outcome: ICU stay** *(Source: Synopsis and Outcome Variables, pages 2, 8)*

- **Definition:** Length of ICU stay. *(Source: Synopsis and Outcome Variables, pages 2, 8)*
- **Measurement Units:** Not reported in protocol. *(Source: pages 1-31 reviewed)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** During ICU admission; no derivation rule is reported. *(Source: pages 1-31 reviewed)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Not reported in protocol. *(Source: pages 1-31 reviewed)*

**Outcome: Mortality** *(Source: Synopsis and Outcome Variables, pages 2, 8)*

- **Definition:** Overall mortality in ICU, in hospital, at day 28, and at month 6. *(Source: Outcome Variables, page 8)*
- **Measurement Units:** Proportion/mortality status; no analytic unit is further reported. *(Source: Outcome Variables, page 8)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** ICU, hospital, day 28, and month 6; after discharge, vital status is assessed by telephone at day 28 and month 6. *(Source: Synopsis and Outcome Variables, pages 3, 8)*
- **Transformations / Derived Variables:** Not reported in protocol. *(Source: pages 1-31 reviewed)*

**Outcome: Day-7 vitamin-D repletion** *(Source: Synopsis and Outcome Variables, pages 2, 8)*

- **Measurement Units:** Percent meeting the 25(OH)D threshold; threshold in ng/mL. *(Source: Synopsis and Outcome Variables, pages 2, 8)*
- **Timing of Measurement:** Day 7. *(Source: Synopsis and Outcome Variables, pages 2, 8)*

**Outcome: Laboratory and inflammatory measures** *(Source: Outcome Variables, pages 8-9)*
